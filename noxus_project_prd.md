# NOXUS: Project Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
**NOXUS** is a modern, student-led technical bootcamp platform designed for high-performance engineering education. Moving away from traditional Learning Management Systems (LMS), NOXUS provides a developer-centric, gamified environment that mirrors professional engineering workflows.

### 1.1 Mission Statement
"Empowering the next generation of engineers through structured discovery, collaborative building, and data-driven growth."

### 1.2 Target Audience
*   **Students:** ~250 first-year engineering students.
*   **Admins/Instructors:** Program leads and technical mentors.

---

## 2. Product Personality & Visual Identity
*   **Personality:** Technical, Modern, Energetic, Professional.
*   **Visual Language:** SaaS-inspired, clean layouts, rounded geometry (8px), and high-fidelity data visualization.
*   **Brand Tokens:**
    *   **Primary:** Deep Navy (#0f172a)
    *   **Accent:** Electric Blue
    *   **Success:** Emerald Green
    *   **Font:** Inter (Sans-serif)

---

## 3. Core Functional Pillars

### 3.1 Explore (Domain Discovery)
*   **Domain Hub:** A curated gallery of engineering specialties (Web, AI, IoT, etc.).
*   **Progress Tracking:** Visual indicators of exploration depth within specific technical tracks.

### 3.2 Learn & Practice (Roadmap & Tasks)
*   **12-Week Roadmap:** A phased journey from "Foundations" to "Capstone Project."
*   **Active Missions:** A prioritized task pipeline (To-Do, In Progress, Review, Completed).
*   **GitHub Integration:** Automatic tracking of developer activity and documentation quality.

### 3.3 Collaborate (Community & Networking)
*   **Blogs Hub:** A knowledge-sharing center for technical guides and student success stories.
*   **LinkedIn Connect:** Seamless professional branding tools to share career milestones.
*   **Peer Reviews:** Structured feedback loops between cohort members.

### 3.4 Measure (Analytics & Gamification)
*   **Progress vs. Rank:** A dual-metric system distinguishing personal mastery from peer standing.
*   **Skill Matrix:** Radar charts visualizing technical proficiency across different domains.
*   **XP & Streaks:** Gamification elements to encourage consistent daily engagement.

---

## 4. Administrative Features
*   **Admin Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into cohort health, engagement trends, and submission rates.
*   **At-Risk Alerts:** Automated identification of students requiring technical or motivational intervention.
*   **Management Suite:** Tools for student auditing, task publishing, and analytics exports.

---

## 5. Technical Requirements
*   **Frontend:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
*   **Architecture:** Component-based, responsive (Mobile-first for tracking, Desktop-first for building).
*   **Integrations:** GitHub API, LinkedIn API, Internal Progress Engine.

---

## 6. Success Metrics
*   **Engagement:** Weekly active users (WAU) and daily streak maintenance.
*   **Completion:** Task and assessment pass rates across the 12-week cycle.
*   **Portfolio Quality:** Number of successfully deployed student projects.
